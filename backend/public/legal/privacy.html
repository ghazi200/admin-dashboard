1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how ABE Security (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when customers and their authorized users use the ABE Guard platform (admin dashboard, guard mobile app, and related APIs, SMS, and voice notifications).

Contact for privacy requests: privacy@abessecurity.example (replace with your production address).

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: names, emails, phone numbers, roles, passwords (hashed), MFA settings.
  • Workforce & scheduling data: shifts, sites/locations, availability, callouts, swap requests, time clock entries, incidents/reports as entered by the customer.
  • Communications: in-app messages, email/SMS/voice delivery metadata for staffing alerts; consent flags and timestamps for SMS/voice.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/app version, logs, cookies or similar for session security.
  • AI processing inputs: operational metrics and prompts used to generate rankings, summaries, or recommendations (no payment card data).

3. How we use information

  • Provide scheduling, callout, messaging, timekeeping, and admin features.
  • Send SMS/voice/email only where the customer or end user has provided required consent and preferences.
  • Secure accounts (including MFA), prevent abuse, and troubleshoot.
  • Improve reliability and AI-assisted operational insights for the customer’s tenant.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

4. Multi-tenant isolation

Customer data is logically separated by organization (tenant). Authorized admins for one tenant are not permitted to access another tenant’s workforce data, subject to super-admin operators acting under our internal controls.

5. Sharing

We share data with:

  • Service providers that host or deliver the service (e.g. cloud hosting, database, email, SMS/voice carriers such as Twilio, optional AI API providers).
  • The customer organization that provisions the account (administrators see data for their tenant).
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as the customer account is active and as needed for legal, security, and backup purposes. Customers may request export or deletion of tenant data subject to the MSA/DPA and applicable law.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords, access controls, optional MFA for admins, and tenant isolation controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your choices

  • Guards may revoke SMS/voice consent (e.g. reply STOP to SMS) and update contact preferences where available.
  • Admins may update or delete guard profiles within the product subject to role permissions.
  • Privacy requests: email the contact above. We may need to verify identity and tenant affiliation.

9. International / processors

Infrastructure may be hosted in the United States (or other regions used by our cloud providers). Where we process personal data on behalf of a customer, the customer is typically the controller and we act as a processor under a DPA.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
