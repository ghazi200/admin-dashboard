SMS & Voice Call Consent

ABE Security / ABE Guard — employee staffing notifications

How we obtain consent

Before ABE Security sends SMS text messages or places phone calls about shift coverage, callouts, or schedule offers, the employee (guard) must provide an affirmative opt-in.

Consent is collected when:

  • A supervisor adds or updates the guard’s mobile number in the ABE Admin Dashboard, and the guard confirms opt-in; and/or
  • The guard enables SMS/voice notifications in the ABE Guard mobile app profile; and/or
  • The guard completes onboarding and checks the communications consent box (shown below).

We store the phone number, consent timestamp, and channel (SMS and/or voice). Consent is optional and may be revoked at any time.

Consent language (shown at opt-in)

I agree that ABE Security may contact me by SMS and/or phone call at the mobile number I provide about shift coverage, callouts, schedule offers, and related staffing notices. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS; reply HELP for help. I can revoke consent anytime by contacting my supervisor or support.

Example SMS

ABE callout MAIN BUILDING 2026-08-04 07:00-15:00. Open Guard app to accept. Reply STOP to opt out.

Example voice call

Automated call from ABE Security describing an open shift and offering keypad options (for example: press 1 to accept, press 2 to decline), or directing the guard to the ABE Guard app.

Opt-out

  • SMS: Reply STOP (START to resume where supported).
  • Voice / all channels: Contact your supervisor or email support to remove consent.

Program: ABE Guard staffing notifications · Sender: ABE Security Twilio number
Questions: contact your ABE Security administrator.
